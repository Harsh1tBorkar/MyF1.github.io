What is Formula 1?

Formula 1 is the highest class of international single‑seater racing, with 20 drivers and 10 teams competing across a global calendar of races each season.

Each race weekend is held on a dedicated circuit or a street track, from classics like Silverstone and Monza to modern venues like Las Vegas and Miami.

Teams

There are 10 teams, each running two cars and two drivers, forming the 20‑car grid.

Drivers

The grid mixes world champions such as Lewis Hamilton and Max Verstappen with rising rookies.

Circuits

F1 visits around two dozen circuits across five continents, from street tracks to permanent raceways.
