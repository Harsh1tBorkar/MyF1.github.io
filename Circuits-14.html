Formula 1 Circuits

The calendar features around two dozen races across five continents, from classic venues like Silverstone and Spa to street tracks such as Monaco and Las Vegas.

Monaco

Narrow city streets and low average speeds but huge driver challenge and prestige.

Monza

High‑speed Italian circuit known as the “Temple of Speed”, with long straights and heavy braking zones.

Spa‑Francorchamps

Long lap in Belgium with big elevation change and famous corners like Eau Rouge.
